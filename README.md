# Mushrooms After Rain

## A compact portrait

A wet week can bring an entire hidden landscape to the surface: caps, cups, fans, corals, and tiny umbrellas.

- **Look closely:** Shelf fungi add a new pale rim as they expand across fallen wood.
- **Look closely:** Waxcaps shine in old grasslands where the soil has remained undisturbed.
- **Look closely:** Many delicate caps last only a day before dissolving back into the leaf litter.

## Why it stays interesting

Photographs and close observation are safer guides than guesswork; many lookalikes differ in important ways.

_Field notes on woodland mushrooms, damp weather, and the fleeting forms that appear after rain._
